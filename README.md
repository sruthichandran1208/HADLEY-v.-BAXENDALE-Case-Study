### Hadley v. Baxendale – Case Study

A case study on **Hadley v. Baxendale**, focusing on breach of contract and the rules for claiming damages. It covers the case facts, legal issue, court decision, and the main principle established by the case. This case helped me understand how damages are decided when a contract is not properly performed.
